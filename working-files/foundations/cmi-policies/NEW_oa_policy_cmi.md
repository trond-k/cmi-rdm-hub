
# CMI Open Access (OA) policy — 2025 update

## 1) Policy statement and scope

* **CMI is a Green Open Access institution.** All written research outputs authored or co-authored by CMI staff must be registered on cmi.no and a full text must be deposited in CMI’s institutional repository. Peer-reviewed journal articles must be made openly available immediately when funder mandates require it. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
* **Funder alignment.** For Research Council of Norway (RCN)–funded projects from calls in 2021 and onward, full and immediate OA is required under Plan S (no embargo), with an open licence that permits reuse. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])

## 2) Licensing and rights

* **Default licence for articles.** Use CC BY 4.0. CC BY-SA or CC0 are permitted alternatives. CC BY-ND may be used only with academic justification reported in the project’s final report. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
* **Rights Retention Strategy (RRS).** To ensure immediate OA even in subscription journals, authors must retain rights in the Author Accepted Manuscript (AAM). Include this text on first submission when RCN-funded:
  “This research was funded, in whole or in part, by The Research Council of Norway \[project number]. For the purpose of open access, the author has applied a CC BY public copyright license to any Author Accepted Manuscript (AAM) version arising from this submission.” ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
* **Institutional RRS encouragement.** CMI encourages adoption of an institutional rights-retention approach (Harvard-style) similar to UiT’s, placing legal responsibility with the institution and ensuring deposit of AAMs/VORs.&#x20;
* **Author addenda.** Where helpful (e.g., non-funded work or publishers unfamiliar with RRS), use the SPARC Author Addendum to keep repository and teaching rights. ([SPARC][2])

## 3) Where and how to publish

* **Prefer national publish-and-read agreements** coordinated by Sikt (openscience.no). These often cover APCs in hybrid journals and sometimes give discounts in fully OA journals. Check whether the journal is covered before submitting. Note that Wiley has **no** national agreement from 2025. ([Open Science][3])
* **Norwegian channel levels.** Journals and publishers should be level 1 or 2 in the Norwegian Register for Scientific Journals, Series and Publishers. ([Kanalregister][4])
* **Check self-archiving rules** for any journal/publisher in Sherpa Romeo to confirm which version you can upload and any conditions. ([Sherpa][5])

## 4) What to deposit, version order, and embargoes

When registering any publication at cmi.no, **always upload a file**, in this order of preference:

1. **Publisher’s PDF** (Version of Record / VOR)
2. **Author’s post-print** (AAM; final peer-reviewed manuscript)
3. **Author’s pre-print** (submitted version)

* **Embargoes.**
  • RCN projects from 2021 calls onward: **no embargo permitted**; deposit AAM or VOR **immediately** with an open licence.
  • For older RCN projects (pre-2021 calls): max embargo 6 months (STEM/health) or 12 months (SSH). Enter the embargo end date at registration. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])

## 5) Books and book chapters

* **RCN-funded academic books must be OA.** Immediate OA is recommended; if an embargo is necessary it must be **≤ 12 months**. Licences may be CC BY, CC BY-ND, or CC BY-NC (or equivalent). Authors/institutions must retain sufficient rights to provide OA and allow reuse. ([Norges forskningsråd][6])
* **Project budgeting.** RCN covers costs for OA books published without embargo as direct project costs. For journal articles, RCN expects OA costs to be handled via institutional overheads or national agreements; **hybrid article fees are not covered** by RCN. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])

## 6) APCs and cost coverage at CMI

* **Main rule.** CMI does **not** pay APCs when self-archiving achieves OA.
* **Eligible exceptions.** CMI may cover APCs for **fully OA (gold)** journals that are level 1 or 2 **when** national agreements do not already cover the fee and the corresponding author is affiliated with CMI; apply to your Research Director. (Hybrid APCs are not supported; use publish-and-read agreements instead.) ([Norges forskningsråd][1])

## 7) Roles and responsibilities

* **Researchers**
  • Register every external output (journal article, book/chapter, report, working paper, blog/op-ed) at cmi.no and **upload a file**.
  • For RCN-funded outputs, include the **RRS statement at submission**, apply **CC BY** to the AAM, and deposit **at acceptance** (metadata) and **at publication** (file available). ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
  • Use Sherpa Romeo to double-check self-archiving terms. ([Sherpa][5])
* **Project owner (CMI)**
  • Ensures RRS and OA compliance for RCN-funded publications and reports any CC BY-ND justifications in the final report. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
  • Provides guidance on use of national agreements and repository deposit.

## 8) Submission checklist (copy/paste for researchers)

* **Before submission**
  • Check journal in **Kanalregisteret** (level 1/2) and whether a **Sikt agreement** applies. ([Kanalregister][4])
  • If RCN-funded, **paste the RRS sentence** into your cover letter or manuscript acknowledgement. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
  • If needed, attach **SPARC Author Addendum**. ([SPARC][2])
* **On acceptance**
  • Deposit the **AAM with CC BY** (or VOR if permitted) in the CMI repository; include grant number and licence metadata. ([Norges forskningsråd][1])
* **On publication**
  • Update the record with the **publisher DOI** and, if the VOR can be shared, replace AAM with the **publisher PDF**.
  • If an embargo applies (older RCN calls or non-RCN), set the embargo end date.
