# Open data:

## Sikt Research Data Archive
**Information:** Sikt accepts and archives research data on people and society, making it accessible for reuse. Norway's national research data archive for social sciences, humanities, and parts of medical and health research. CoreTrustSeal certified and Norwegian service provider to CESSDA ERIC. Has an agreement to archive research data on behalf of the National Archives of Norway since 2014. Main focus is quantitative data matrices on individuals, organizations, administrative, political, and geographical actors.

**Suitable for:** survey data (provides survey level data documentation)

**How they align to FAIR:**
- **Findable:** All data available in the Sikt Data Catalogue in accordance with FAIR principles; searchable and citable data published in Surveybanken; metadata harvesting for discovery
- **Accessible:** Data safeguarded and accessible for at least 10 years, with long-term preservation of at least 50 years for high-value curated data; data shared as openly as possible, as closed as necessary
- **Interoperable:** Uses DDI-Lifecycle 3.3 metadata standard for archiving; complies with CESSDA Metadata Model specifications; operates with international research data archive practices
- **Reusable:** Five curation levels (1-4 meet FAIR principles); extensive variable-level curation; data prepared for secondary use in research; quality-controlled and documented to enhance reusability

- **DMP Compliance Statement**: Research data will be deposited in Sikt Research Data Archive, a CoreTrustSeal-certified repository that ensures FAIR compliance through DDI-Lifecycle 3.3 metadata standards, guaranteed 10-year accessibility with up to 50-year preservation for curated datasets, and professional curation services meeting Norwegian national archiving requirements and CESSDA European standards.

## OSF
**Information**: Free, open-source project management platform by Center for Open Science supporting the entire research lifecycle. FAIR-compliant repository with built-in version control, DOI assignment through DataCite, and integration capabilities with GitHub, Dropbox, Google Drive. Provides up to 50GB free storage for public projects.

**Suitable for**: replication data / packages

**How they align to FAIR**:

- **Findable**: Globally Unique Identifiers (GUIDs) for all objects; DOI registration with DataCite; ORCID integration; free text search functionality.

- **Accessible**: Open access without account requirement for public content; request access and private sharing settings available; API access for programmatic retrieval

- **Interoperable**: Integration with external tools (GitHub, Zotero, etc.); standardized metadata formats; API for custom tool integration.

- **Reusable**: License picker for various CC licenses; detailed documentation requirements; version control; rich metadata requirements.

**DMP Compliance Statement**: Research data and materials will be shared via the Open Science Framework (OSF), which provides FAIR-compliant data sharing with DataCite DOI assignment, comprehensive version control, flexible licensing options, and seamless integration with analysis tools, ensuring reproducibility and meeting open science mandates from major research funders.

# Open Articles and other output:

## Vitenarkivet / Norwegian Research Information Repository (NVA)
**Information:** Norway's national research information system and open digital research archive, replacing Cristin and institutional repositories. Used to register publications, projects and research activities while making scholarly publications, theses openly accessible. Supports Norway's goal for all publicly funded scientific articles to be openly accessible.

**Suitable for:** Plan S compliance

**How it supports Plan S:** Enables self-archiving of author accepted manuscripts; supports rights retention strategies; provides immediate open access to research outputs; meets Plan S requirements for publicly funded research accessibility

**DMP Compliance Statement:** All research publications will be deposited in the Norwegian Research Information Repository (NVA) to ensure compliance with Norwegian national open access policies, Plan S requirements, and Research Council of Norway mandates, providing immediate open access, persistent identifiers, and integration with national research reporting systems.

## CMI Institutional repository  
**Information:**
- Local institutional repository for CMI research outputs
- Integration with NVA for national reporting requirements
- Supports institutional open access policies and mandates
- Provides persistent URLs and basic metadata for institutional publications
- Handles institutional theses, reports, working papers, and grey literature
- Supports embargo periods for articles with publisher restrictions
- Integration with CMI website and research profile pages
- Backup and preservation services for institutional scholarly record

**DMP Compliance Statement:** Institutional research outputs including theses, reports, and working papers will be deposited in CMI's institutional repository, which provides persistent identification, basic FAIR metadata, embargo support for publisher agreements, automated NVA integration for national reporting compliance, and long-term digital preservation of the institutional scholarly record.

## Additional Recommended Repositories

### **Figshare**
**Information:** Generalist repository by Digital Science supporting all research outputs and file types. Free service with up to many TB support via Figshare+. ISO27001 certified and endorsed TRUST and FAIR principles. Founded 2011, serves 150+ institutional clients globally with guaranteed 10-year minimum retention.

**Suitable for:** General research outputs, figures, datasets, media, presentations, and filesets requiring flexible licensing and anonymous peer review capabilities

**How they align to FAIR:**
- **Findable:** DOI assignment via DataCite; rich mandatory metadata including title, authors, keywords; OAI-PMH API support
- **Accessible:** HTTPS access; private sharing links for peer review; embargo and access restriction options; API for programmatic access
- **Interoperable:** Supports 1,200+ file types with browser preview; JSON-LD metadata; standard citation formats
- **Reusable:** CC0/CC-BY licensing for data, GPL/MIT/Apache for code; detailed provenance tracking; altmetric scores and usage statistics

**DMP Compliance Statement:** Research data and outputs will be shared via Figshare, an ISO27001-certified repository providing FAIR compliance through DataCite DOIs, comprehensive metadata standards, flexible licensing, version control, and guaranteed 10-year preservation, meeting funder requirements and supporting anonymous peer review processes.

### **Harvard Dataverse**
**Information:** Open-source repository software with 60,000+ datasets, developed by Harvard Library, HUIT, and IQSS. Free up to 1TB for all researchers worldwide. Part of global network with dozens of institutional installations.

**Suitable for:** Quantitative and qualitative research data requiring hierarchical organization, collaborative features, and advanced curation

**How they align to FAIR:**
- **Findable:** DOI assignment to datasets and files; ORCID integration; advanced search and text mining capabilities
- **Accessible:** Open access, restricted, and embargo options; request access features; multiple export formats
- **Interoperable:** Standard-compliant metadata mapping to common schemas; tabular data integration; API access
- **Reusable:** CC0 encouraged; custom terms support; download monitoring; comprehensive provenance tracking

**DMP Compliance Statement:** Research data will be deposited in Harvard Dataverse, a trusted open-source repository providing FAIR-compliant data sharing through DOI assignment, hierarchical data organization, standard metadata schemas, flexible access controls, and long-term preservation within a global network of academic institutions.

### **GitHub + Zenodo Integration**
**Information:** GitHub provides version control and collaboration for research software. Integration with Zenodo enables automatic DOI assignment and long-term archival. Supports FAIR4RS (FAIR Principles for Research Software) with persistent identifiers and rich metadata.

**Suitable for:** Research code, software, computational notebooks, and reproducible research workflows

**How they align to FAIR4RS:**
- **Findable:** Globally unique persistent identifiers via DOIs; rich metadata through README, CITATION.cff files; searchable and indexable
- **Accessible:** HTTPS access; clear licensing; detailed development information in public repositories
- **Interoperable:** Standard data types for input/output; documented functional annotations; dependency management
- **Reusable:** Open source licensing (MIT, Apache, GPL); comprehensive documentation; citation files; version control

**DMP Compliance Statement:** Research software and computational workflows will be developed on GitHub with automated archival to Zenodo, ensuring FAIR4RS compliance through version control, persistent DOI assignment, comprehensive documentation, open source licensing, and long-term preservation of executable research assets.

### **re3data.org**
**Information:** Global registry of 3,000+ research data repositories covering all academic disciplines. Provides detailed repository characteristics and certification status to help researchers identify appropriate domain-specific repositories.

**Suitable for:** Repository discovery and selection tool rather than a repository itself

**Utility:** Essential resource for identifying discipline-specific repositories, certified repositories, and repositories meeting specific technical or policy requirements

**DMP Compliance Statement:** Repository selection will be guided by re3data.org registry to identify the most appropriate domain-specific, certified repositories that meet FAIR principles and specific data management requirements for each research project component.
