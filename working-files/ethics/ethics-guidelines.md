# Ethics Guidelines Catalogue

*Last updated: 24 February 2026*

A curated collection of ethics guidelines, codes of conduct, and regulatory guidance relevant to research at CMI. Entries are grouped by topic and link directly to the source document. This catalogue is maintained and updated as new resources become available.

---

## Norwegian Legal & Regulatory Framework

| Guideline | Description |
|---|---|
| [Norwegian Act on Ethics and Integrity in Research (2017)](https://www.forskningsetikk.no/en/resources/the-research-ethics-library/legal-statutes-and-guidelines/the-act-on-ethics-and-integrity-in-research/) | The foundational Norwegian law defining responsibilities of institutions and researchers for ensuring research is conducted according to recognised ethical norms, including handling of misconduct. |
| [Norwegian Personal Data Act (Personopplysningsloven, 2018)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2018-06-15-38) | Norway's implementation of GDPR into national law, supplementing the regulation with Norwegian-specific provisions on research (§§ 8–9), data protection officers, and enforcement. |
| [Norwegian National Strategy on Access to and Sharing of Research Data](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/dokumenter/national-strategy-on-access-to-and-sharing-of-research-data/id2582412/) | The Norwegian government's national strategy establishing that publicly funded research data should be open by default, curated for reuse, and managed according to FAIR principles. |

## Research Integrity & Codes of Conduct

| Guideline | Description |
|---|---|
| [ALLEA European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity (2023)](https://allea.org/portfolio-item/european-code-of-conduct-2023/) | Commission-recognised 2023 European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity: principles, good practices, and handling of breaches. |
| [European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity (ALLEA) — Norwegian adaptation](https://www.forskningsetikk.no/en/resources/the-european-code-of-conduct-for-research-integrity/) | Revised European-wide code for research integrity covering principles, practices and violations, adapted into Norwegian by national research ethics committees. |
| [TRUST Code: Global Code of Conduct for Equitable Research Partnerships](https://www.globalcodeofconduct.org/) | Global code targeting ethics dumping and helicopter research, ensuring fairness, respect, care and honesty in international research partnerships. |
| [NESH Guidelines for Research Ethics in Social Sciences and Humanities](https://www.forskningsetikk.no/en/guidelines/social-sciences-humanities-law-and-theology/) | National Committee for Research Ethics guidelines tailored to social sciences, humanities, law and theology, including international collaboration considerations. |
| [NESH Guide to Internet Research Ethics (2019)](https://www.forskningsetikk.no/en/guidelines/social-sciences-and-humanities/a-guide-to-internet-research-ethics/) | NESH guide addressing ethical considerations when conducting research using internet-based data, including accessibility, sensitivity, and interaction with participants online. |
| [AAA Principles of Professional Responsibility](https://ethics.americananthro.org/category/statement/) | American Anthropological Association's seven ethical principles covering do no harm, informed consent, competing obligations, and respectful relationships in ethnographic research. |
