## When to file

Sikt notification is required for any research project that processes personal data. Since most CMI research involves identifiable participants, most CMI projects will need a Sikt notification. File the notification **at least 30 days before the planned start of data collection**. Researchers should not begin data processing until Sikt has completed its assessment.

The notification is **not an approval** — Sikt does not approve or reject research designs. It is a notification that data processing will take place, accompanied by a description of the processing and the safeguards in place. Sikt's role is to assess whether the described processing is consistent with data protection requirements and to advise the institution if it identifies concerns. However, the assessment may identify issues that must be addressed before processing can lawfully begin.


## CMI's institutional agreement with Sikt

CMI has an institutional agreement with Sikt (formerly NSD) for privacy services in research (*personverntjenester for forskning*). Under this agreement:

- **Designated contact**: CMI has a designated institutional contact person and access to Sikt's institutional portal (*Institusjonsportalen*), which provides an overview of all notified projects and their status.
- **GDPR records of processing**: the Sikt notification system serves as CMI's practical mechanism for maintaining records of processing activities as required by GDPR Article 30. This makes timely and accurate notification important not only for the individual project but for CMI's institutional compliance.
- **Ongoing support**: Sikt provides guidance throughout the project lifecycle, including at project conclusion. Researchers can communicate with their assigned Sikt adviser through the *minforskning.sikt.no* portal.
- **Breach notification**: Sikt will alert CMI without undue delay if it suspects or discovers a breach of data protection rules during its assessments.
- **Internal guide**: CMI has a Sikt Notification Form Guide with step-by-step instructions for researchers on how to fill out the notification form.


## What to include in the notification

Include in the Sikt notification:

- Description of the project and its purpose
- Lawful basis: **public interest (Article 6(1)(e))** — CMI's default (see `gdpr-positions.md`)
- For special category data: Article 9(2)(j)
- Types of personal data collected
- Data subjects (who the participants are)
- How data is collected and stored (see `data-security.md` for storage by classification tier)
- Who has access
- Retention period (see `retention.md` for default periods)
- Whether data will be shared with partners or transferred abroad (see `partnerships.md`)
- Safeguards (encryption, access controls, anonymisation plans)

### What Sikt assesses

Sikt evaluates whether the planned processing:
- Has a legal basis
- Complies with data protection principles (lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation)
- Provides adequate information to data subjects
- Respects data subject rights

Sikt also provides general guidance on data security, data sharing arrangements, and cross-border data transfers.


## Institutional defaults for CMI notifications

The following apply to all CMI notifications unless the project's specific circumstances justify a different approach:

- **Lawful basis**: public interest (Article 6(1)(e)), supported by the Norwegian Personal Data Act §8
- **Data controller**: CMI (Chr. Michelsen Institute)
- **Data protection services**: provided by Sikt under CMI's institutional agreement

**Action for the researcher**: file the notification during project setup, at least 30 days before data collection. Use CMI's Sikt Notification Form Guide as a starting point. Allow time for Sikt's review before data collection starts.
