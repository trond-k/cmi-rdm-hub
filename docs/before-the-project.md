# Before the project

*The decisions you make before data collection begins set the trajectory for everything that follows. These three stages establish the intellectual, administrative, and procedural foundations for data management. Getting this right reduces friction, cost, and risk throughout the lifecycle.*

## Three stages, one goal

The 'before' phase covers three distinct stages, each with its own focus:

| Stage | Focus | Key outcome |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| [**Frame**](lifecycle-1-frame.md) | Design and conceptualise | A clear picture of what data you need, whether it exists, and what handling it will involve |
| [**Fund**](lifecycle-2-fund.md) | Align with funder requirements | A proposal that commits to realistic data management and budgets for it |
| [**Plan**](lifecycle-3-plan.md) | Formalise data handling | A working Data Management Plan with roles, responsibilities, and legal groundwork in place |

These stages overlap in practice. You may start planning before the funding decision, or revisit your framing after a funder requests changes. The lifecycle is a guide, not a rigid sequence.

## Common questions at this stage

??? question "Do I need a Data Management Plan before I apply for funding?"
    Not a full DMP, but your proposal should demonstrate that you have thought about data management. Most funders expect a data management strategy in the application, and some (RCN, Horizon Europe) require a DMP within the first months of the project. See [Fund](lifecycle-2-fund.md) for funder-specific requirements.

??? question "How do I know if my project involves personal data?"
    See [Decide whether your project uses personal data](personal-data-decider.md) for a detailed guide. The definition is broader than most researchers expect: it includes any information that can be linked to an identifiable person, directly or indirectly. Audio recordings, IP addresses logged by survey platforms, and combinations of background variables in small populations all count.

??? question "When should I contact Sikt?"
    Before data collection begins, if your project involves personal data. See [Prepare a Sikt notification](sikt-notification.md) for what to have ready before you start the form.
