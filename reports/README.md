# Funder Requirements: Research Reports

**Compiled: 2026-03-26**

These reports document the current data management, open access, and open science requirements for the four funders referenced in the FUND lifecycle stage. They are research outputs intended to inform the CMI RDM guidance hub -- not published guidance themselves. Always verify against current call documentation before advising researchers.

## Key observations

### Convergence across RCN, Horizon Europe, and ERC

All three research funders have converged on essentially the same framework:

- **"As open as possible, as closed as necessary"** as the governing principle for research data.
- **Immediate open access** to publications with no embargo (Plan S for RCN; MGA Article 17 for Horizon Europe/ERC).
- **FAIR data management** as a binding or near-binding obligation.
- **CC BY** as the default licence for publications; **CC BY or CC0** for data.
- **Metadata must always be open**, even when data is restricted.
- **DMPs as living documents**, due within six months of project start and updated at project end.
- **Data management costs are eligible** in project budgets.

The practical differences are minor: RCN uses a 9-area DMP guide based on Science Europe; Horizon Europe uses a 7-section template. RCN does not assess DMP content (institutional responsibility); Horizon Europe treats open science as part of proposal evaluation. But for a CMI researcher writing a proposal, the core obligations are functionally identical.

### Norad as the outlier

Norad's framework is structurally different from the research funders:

- **No formal DMP requirement.** Norad asks for a Knowledge Management Plan instead, which serves a related but distinct function.
- **No FAIR mandate.** The concept does not appear in Norad's grant conditions.
- **IP ownership stays with the grant recipient** (CMI), but Norad retains a royalty-free, assignable licence.
- **The new Open Policy** (effective January 2025) signals a shift toward structured openness, but it currently applies only to the Department for Climate, Nature, and the Private Sector -- not all Norad-funded work.
- **The grant vs. commission distinction is critical.** Under grants, CMI retains IP and publication rights. Under commissions (procurement), IP may transfer to Norad, and publication timing may be restricted. Contract terms vary.

### Shared infrastructure covers all four funders

CMI's existing infrastructure aligns with requirements across all funders:

| Need | CMI default |
|---|---|
| Data archiving | Sikt Research Data Archive (CoreTrustSeal, meets RCN/HE/ERC "trusted repository" criteria) |
| Publication archiving | NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv) |
| DMP drafting | Sikt DMP tool (plan.research-data.no), with templates for both RCN and Horizon Europe |
| Sensitive data storage | TSD (during active research) |
| Active storage | Microsoft 365 E5 |

### Gaps worth noting

- **Norad has no standardised contract template for commissions** that is publicly available. Terms vary by engagement, making it harder to give blanket guidance.
- **Norad evaluation data archiving is undefined.** Evaluation reports are published, but underlying datasets (surveys, qualitative data) have no systematic preservation pathway.
- **RCN does not assess DMP quality.** This places the burden on CMI as Project Owner to ensure DMPs meet institutional standards before submission.
- **ERC Proof of Concept grants are exempt** from open research data requirements -- the only carve-out among these funders.
