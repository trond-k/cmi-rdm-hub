# Research Council of Norway (RCN): Data Management and Open Science Requirements

**Compiled: 2026-03-26 | For: CMI RDM Guidance Hub**

---

## 1. Data Management Plan (DMP) Requirements

### Who must submit a DMP?

All projects that received funding after **1 January 2018** and that manage data must submit a DMP. The Project Owner must assess the need for a DMP for every funded project; if no plan is needed, a written explanation is required.

Source: [Sharing research data](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/research-data/)

### When is it due?

- **First version**: when revising the grant application (i.e., after award, at the contract stage). The DMP should be established at an early phase of the project, preferably within six months of start-up.
- **Updated version**: together with the project's final report.
- The DMP is a **living document** that should be regularly updated throughout the project.

### What template/format?

- RCN's guide is based on the **Science Europe "Practical Guide to the International Alignment of Research Data Management"**.
- File format: PDF, .doc(x), or similar. RCN is developing support for machine-actionable DMPs following the RDA Common Standard.
- Recommended tools: **Sikt DMP** (plan.research-data.no), Data Stewardship Wizard (ELIXIR Norway), easyDMP, Argos (OpenAIRE), Digital Curation Centre.

Source: [plan.research-data.no DMP requirements](https://plan.research-data.no/pages/support_00_dmp_requirements)

### What must it cover? (Nine areas in the RCN guide)

1. **Administrative information** -- Institution, project manager, project number, title, funder, version
2. **Data collection/reuse** -- Methods, software, provenance, format limitations, rationale
3. **File types and formats** -- Data types (numerical, text, image, audio, video), storage formats, estimated volume
4. **Documentation and metadata** -- Metadata standards (DDI, TEI, MARC, CMDI), file organisation, version control, naming conventions, quality control
5. **Storage and security** -- Storage location, backup frequency, disaster recovery, access controls, sensitive data handling
6. **Rights and legal requirements** -- Rights holders, licences, GDPR/Personal Data Act compliance, informed consent for long-term preservation, anonymisation/pseudonymisation, multi-partner coordination, ethics
7. **Data sharing and reuse** -- Discoverability, repositories, timing of sharing, access restrictions, persistent identifiers (DOIs)
8. **Long-term preservation** -- Retention criteria, selection rationale, preservation location and duration
9. **Responsibilities and resources** -- Role assignments, partner coordination, budget allocation for curation, storage, staffing, repository costs

### Assessment of DMPs

**RCN does not assess the content of submitted DMPs.** It is the Project Owner's responsibility to approve that the plan is in line with the institution's requirements and guidelines before submission.

DMPs should, as far as possible, be **public and published openly** to enable communities to share best practices.

## 2. Open Access to Research Data

### Default policy

> **"As open as possible, as closed as necessary."**

RCN's *Policy for Open Access to Research Data* (originally adopted 2014, revised 2017) aims to ensure that research data are accessible to relevant users, on equal terms, and at the lowest possible cost.

Source: [RCN Policy on Open Science](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/policy/)

### When must data be shared?

Data should be shared **at the latest when scientific publications rely on the data**, though earlier sharing is encouraged.

### Exceptions (data may remain closed when)

- It would **threaten individual or national security**
- It **conflicts with data protection regulations** (GDPR, Personal Data Act)
- It **violates other legal provisions**
- **Exclusive rights** may temporarily delay sharing to protect intellectual property or patents (must be justified)

Non-commercial-only usage or other purpose limitations may be applied with documented rationale.

Source: [Sharing research data](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/research-data/)

## 3. Open Access to Publications

### Journal articles (Plan S)

RCN was a founding member of **cOAlition S** when Plan S launched in autumn 2018. Requirements apply to **projects with funding from calls for proposals in 2021 and onwards**.

**Core requirement**: Full and immediate open access -- no embargo allowed.

Source: [Open access to publications](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/publications/)

### Three compliance routes

1. **Gold OA**: Publish in a fully open access journal or platform.
2. **Green OA (self-archiving)**: Deposit the peer-reviewed version (AAM or VoR) in an open repository at the time of publication. No embargo permitted. Must carry a CC licence.
3. **Transformative agreements**: Publish in a journal covered by an institutional transformative agreement.

**Hybrid journals** (subscription journals offering individual article OA for a fee) are **not funded by RCN**.

The **Journal Checker Tool** (integrated with Norway's scientific journal registry) can verify compliance.

Source: [Plan S and RCN -- UiO Library](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/writing-publishing/open-access/plan-s/)

### Licensing requirements

| Licence | Status |
|---|---|
| **CC BY 4.0** | Default / preferred |
| **CC BY-SA** | Permitted alternative |
| **CC0** | Permitted alternative |
| **CC BY-ND** | Permitted only with academic justification in the final report |

### Rights retention statement

When submitting a manuscript, researchers must include:

> *"This research was funded, in whole or in part, by The Research Council of Norway [6-digit project number]. For open access, the author has applied a CC BY public copyright licence to any Author Accepted Manuscript (AAM) version arising from this submission, in accordance with the grant's open access conditions."*

### Funder acknowledgement (all outputs)

All publications and external activities must state:

> *"This research is/was funded, wholly or partly, by the Research Council of Norway, project number [6-digit number]."*

### Pre-2021 projects

Projects funded before 2021 have different requirements with **6--12 month embargo periods** depending on discipline (not immediate OA).

### Academic books

Guidelines for open access to academic books apply to **calls from 2023 onwards**:

- Monographs and anthology chapters must be made OA.
- Maximum **12 months embargo** after publication.
- Publication costs for OA books/chapters may be included as **direct project costs** -- but only for books published OA immediately (books with an embargo period cannot be funded through project costs).

Source: [RCN guidelines for OA to academic books (PDF)](https://www.forskningsradet.no/contentassets/68f87c2b3b8f4ef58d83943f2047081a/the-research-council-of-norways-guidelines-for-open-access-to-academic-books.pdf)

### Cost coverage for articles

- **Gold OA APCs**: Covered via institutional overhead, not direct project budgets.
- **Hybrid OA**: Not funded by RCN.
- **Green OA**: Free (self-archiving in institutional/subject repositories).

## 4. FAIR Data Principles

RCN **explicitly references FAIR**. From the policy:

> *"The management of research data in projects that receive funding from the Research Council must follow the FAIR principles, as much as possible."*

FAIR = **F**indable, **A**ccessible, **I**nteroperable, **R**eusable.

This is framed as a strong expectation ("must...as much as possible"), not a binary pass/fail requirement. The DMP is the vehicle for demonstrating FAIR compliance through:

- Use of persistent identifiers (DOIs) for datasets and metadata
- Adoption of recognised metadata standards
- Selection of repositories that support FAIR (e.g., certified repositories with CoreTrustSeal)
- Sharing protocols, methods, and source code alongside data

Source: [Sharing research data](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/research-data/)

## 5. Data Sharing and Archiving

### Repository selection

**Primary principle**: The research-performing institution decides which archiving solution to use. However, RCN **may mandate** storage in specific national or international archives -- this will always be stated in the call for proposals and written into the project contract.

### Key Norwegian repositories

| Repository | Scope | Certification |
|---|---|---|
| **Sikt Research Data Archive** (formerly NSD) | Social sciences, humanities, health; also general | CoreTrustSeal |
| **DataverseNO** (UiT/NTNU) | All disciplines, openly accessible data | CoreTrustSeal |
| **NVA** (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv) | Publications (articles, outputs) | National infrastructure |
| **TSD** (UiO) | Sensitive personal data (active storage) | -- |

Repositories should provide persistent identifiers, metadata standards compliance, and sustainability assurances.

### Long-term storage requirement

From RCN's *General Terms and Conditions for R&D Projects* (in effect since 2000):

> The Project Owner shall store the final report and project data in a safe and secure manner for **at least 10 years** after the conclusion of the contract period.

The Project Owner must provide the name of the archives or data infrastructure to be used for storing research data/output data and all necessary documentation for reuse (metadata).

### Timing

Data should be shared no later than when scientific publications rely on it. Exclusive use periods to protect IP or patents may delay sharing temporarily, with justification.

## 6. Eligible Costs

### Data management costs

RCN accepts **any costs for managing data as part of the operating costs** of funded projects, based on the submitted DMP. Eligible costs include:

- **Storage infrastructure and hardware**
- **Staff time** dedicated to data management
- **Data preparation and curation** for sharing
- **Long-term preservation** repository fees
- **Disposal preparation** costs
- **Data collection and fieldwork** costs

When applying for funding, costs for archiving, making available, and sharing data **can be included in the application budget**.

Source: [Sharing research data](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/research-data/), [Budget guidance](https://www.forskningsradet.no/finansiering/hvordan-skrive-soknad/budsjett/)

### Publication costs

- **OA book/chapter publication fees**: Eligible as direct project costs (for immediate OA only, not embargoed publications).
- **Journal article APCs**: Covered via institutional overhead, not direct project budgets.
- **Hybrid OA fees**: Not eligible.

### Infrastructure funding

RCN also funds robust data storage and management infrastructures through the **National Financing Initiative for Research Infrastructure** (INFRASTRUKTUR programme).

## 7. Monitoring and Compliance

### Assessment of open science in applications (from 2023)

Since 2023, **open science practices are assessed by referees** for Researcher Projects and Collaborative and Knowledge-building Projects. Assessment occurs through two subsections of the **"Impact" criterion**:

**Criterion 1 -- Potential impact:**
> "The extent to which the planned outputs are openly accessible to ensure reusability of the research outputs and enhance reproducibility."

Referees examine: FAIR data practices, plans for data sharing, pre-registration and preprints, open access to software/workflows/tools.

**Criterion 2 -- Communication and exploitation:**
> "The extent to which appropriate open science practices are implemented as an integral part of the proposed project to ensure open sharing and wide distribution of research outputs."

Referees examine: early/open sharing strategies, preprint/pre-registration platforms, archiving solutions, compliance with immediate OA mandate.

This aligns with **Horizon Europe** assessment criteria, and many referees have experience from EU evaluations.

Source: [Assessment of open science in grant applications](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/financing/application-process/assessment-open-science/)

### Reporting requirements

- **DMP submission**: First version at contract revision, updated version with final report.
- **Publication tracking**: RCN follows up registration through project reporting throughout the project period.
- **Aggregated compliance data**: RCN regularly publishes figures indicating the degree of compliance with guidelines.
- **CC BY-ND justification**: If CC BY-ND is used instead of CC BY, this must be justified by the project manager in the final report.

### Institutional responsibility

The **Project Owner** (institution) is responsible for:
- Approving that the DMP aligns with institutional requirements
- Ensuring researchers retain the right to self-archive peer-reviewed versions of articles
- Compliance with data protection legislation
- Safe storage of project data for at least 10 years

## 8. Recent Policy Changes (2024--2026)

| Date | Change |
|---|---|
| **Feb 2025** | RCN research data sharing page last revised (13 Feb 2025) |
| **Mar 2025** | Updated *General Terms and Conditions for R&D Projects* -- new terms apply to applications received from 1 March 2025 |
| **Jan 2025** | Quarantine thresholds adjusted downward (fewer PIs receive quarantine) |
| **2023** | Open access to academic books guidelines take effect (for calls from 2023) |
| **2023** | Open science assessment integrated into referee evaluation of Researcher Projects and Collaborative/Knowledge-building Projects |
| **2026** | New rules for personnel and indirect costs: RCN discontinues lump-sum rates for financing researcher time and PhD candidates in the university/college sector and health enterprises. Actual costs become the basis for calculating project support for new calls in 2026 |
| **Ongoing** | Development of machine-actionable DMP support (RDA Common Standard) |
| **Ongoing** | Norwegian coordination of EOSC activities (Ministry of Education and Research + RCN) |

As of 2025, the OA share of articles from Norwegian research is approximately **80%**.

Source: [Open Science](https://www.forskningsradet.no/en/research-policy-strategy/open-science/), [MDPI Blog -- Open Access in Norway](https://blog.mdpi.com/2025/03/25/open-access-in-norway/)

## CMI-Specific Notes

For CMI as a research institute (instituttsektoren):

- **Budget model**: Institute sector personnel and indirect costs follow specific RCN rates (see [kostnader instituttsektoren](https://www.forskningsradet.no/finansiering/hvordan-skrive-soknad/budsjett/kostnader-instituttsektoren/)).
- **Default archive**: Sikt Research Data Archive aligns with both RCN expectations and CMI's existing infrastructure.
- **DMP tool**: Sikt DMP (plan.research-data.no) is the natural choice, already in CMI's toolset.
- **Publication archive**: NVA (Nasjonalt vitenarkiv) for self-archiving articles.
- **10-year storage obligation**: Project Owner (CMI) must ensure safe storage for at least 10 years post-contract -- the DMP should specify how this will be achieved.
- **Sensitive data**: For projects involving personal data, Sikt notification (not IRB approval) and DPIA where required; TSD for sensitive personal data during active research.
